good questions

Things people often ask

Do you charge buyers anything for your service?

No — not a rupee. As authorised channel partners, we're compensated by the developers after a successful transaction. Your property price remains the same as going direct, and often better thanks to our partner offers.

How is a channel partner different from a regular broker?

Brokers chase listings; we represent developers' projects directly. That means verified inventory, transparent pricing, priority unit selection, and proper after-sales support — with zero brokerage for you.

How do you choose which projects to recommend?

We audit every project for RERA compliance, developer track record, construction quality, legal title and location fundamentals. Fewer than 1 in 5 projects we evaluate makes it to our showcase.

Can you help with home loans and documentation?

Absolutely. We have tie-ups with 12+ banks for pre-approved rates, and our team guides you through agreements, stamp duty, registration and society handover — at no extra cost.

I'm an NRI. Can I buy remotely through you?

Yes — our NRI desk handles live video tours, Power of Attorney coordination, legal verification, and registration support. We've helped 400+ NRI families buy with complete confidence.
